FÜR ELISE • L. VAN BEETHOVEN

Complete opening A section of Für Elise, with the engraved repeats and both endings, including the original right-hand notes and quieter left-hand accompaniment. The later B and C sections are not included. The machine has 25 chromatic tuned bars from C4 through C6 in two staggered rows. The original high E6 note sounds at its true pitch but uses the E5 paddle visually. A musical clock drives both the sound and the marbles, so each impact lands on the beat. The hardwood frame, brass gears, exposed piano strings and long upper rail are illustrated mechanisms. Marbles roll along the upper rail, then the timed brass paddles divert each one into its own curved, twin-wire brass guide. The marble remains visible and reaches its tuned bar on the musical beat. The original paddle return springs, eccentric cams, and shared drive shaft remain in place. Rotating eccentric cams, follower rods and return springs illustrate the action; note timing stays locked to the audio. Quiet paddle and elevator clicks use the same musical clock, with adjustable mechanical volume. Inside the lower chamber of this same hardwood cabinet, four violin strings have miniature bows and finger stops driven in time with the marble notes through a shared brass drive linkage.
