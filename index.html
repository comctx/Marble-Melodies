Extended melodic arrangement (not the complete composition). A musical clock drives both the sound and the marbles, so each impact lands on the beat. The hardwood frame, brass gears, exposed piano strings and long upper rail are illustrated mechanisms. Marbles roll across the shared ramp; timed brass paddles have visible return springs and connect to rotating eccentric cams on a shared drive shaft by follower rods, swinging to divert marbles into the correct drop channels. Rotating eccentric cams, follower rods and return springs illustrate the action; note timing stays locked to the audio. Tap Play to enable sound.
