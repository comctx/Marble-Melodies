Für Elise: complete opening A section, with the original right-hand notes and quieter left-hand accompaniment. The later B and C sections are not included; high E6 sounds at its true pitch but uses the E5 paddle visually.

Choose from fifteen songs above. Below the violin are a visible bass drum, twin-stick snare, and brass cymbal with cam-driven beaters. The walnut bass drum has a felt beater and tension rods; the wood-shell snare alternates its sticks; the lathed bronze cymbal flexes and settles after each strike. All contacts follow the audio clock. They perform automatically only when a song calls for drums; no drum button is needed. Für Elise, Amazing Grace, The Entertainer, America the Beautiful and My Country, ’Tis of Thee stay quiet, while Can-Can has lively dance percussion, Battle Hymn, Joy to the World, Deck the Halls, Jingle Bells and We Wish You a Merry Christmas have gentle percussion, and the original Swing Machine has a lively big-band drum arrangement. The machine has 25 chromatic tuned bars from C4 through C6 in two staggered rows. A musical clock drives both the sound and the marbles, so each impact lands on the beat. The recessed walnut cabinet, bolted iron corner braces, bearing mounts, brass gears, exposed piano strings and long upper rail are illustrated mechanisms. Marbles roll along the upper rail, then the timed brass paddles divert each one into its own curved, twin-wire brass guide. The marble remains visible and reaches its tuned bar on the musical beat. The original paddle return springs, eccentric cams, and shared drive shaft remain in place. Rotating eccentric cams, follower rods and return springs illustrate the action; note timing stays locked to the audio. Quiet paddle and elevator clicks use the same musical clock, with adjustable mechanical volume. Inside the lower chamber of the same cabinet, each marble's strike moves a key follower into a four-way brass selector, whose connecting rods operate the appropriate violin bow and finger stop. Both the violin sound and the visible mechanisms use that same exact note-impact clock; the violin's string vibration is simulated. Select Soft duet to layer a gentle violin over the piano, Violin lead to hear bowed-string melody with piano accompaniment, or Off for the original machine. The violin sound is locally synthesized, not a recording. Choose Off, Small Concert Room or Concert Hall reverb for the music while retaining crisp, dry mechanical clicks.
